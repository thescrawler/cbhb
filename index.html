About

The Columbia Basin Honey Badgers are people around the Columbia Basin who want to be ready for their families and their town. Mesh radios talk from radio to radio. They do not need a cell tower for a message to move across the mesh.

This website is the one link to share. It says who we are, how to help test the Android app, where the app and firmware downloads live, and where to look at radios that reach the public map.
