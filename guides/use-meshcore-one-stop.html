Guide

Use MeshCore One Stop

MeshCore One Stop is the Android companion for your MeshCore radio. Package name com.cbhb.meshcoreonestop. These steps point at the longer notes on the app page. They do not add a download.

1. Get the app when the test is open

Public listings on Google Play, GitHub releases, and F-Droid are not up yet. The closed test is not open yet either. When it is, start at Become a tester. This site does not host an install file.

2. Connect your radio

The app talks to the radio over Bluetooth, a USB cable, or TCP (a network address). Chat only moves while that connection is up. Details: Install and pair.

3. Name it on the phone, if you want

A local display name such as “C.B.H.B. Node (1)” or “C.B.H.B. Node (Patterson)” is only on your phone. The on-air name stays as it is. Details: My Devices.

4. Read voltage when you ask

Open My Devices, or pull down to refresh. You get voltage and the trend. The app does not keep checking in the background. The battery percent is estimated from that voltage. Details: Telemetry.

5. Save a log or settings, if you need them

Settings export leaves out passwords and the private key. A CSV log is per device and stays on the phone until you share the file. Details: Export and logs.

6. Treat the map and the messages as two paths

Messages need the radio connection. Map layers may download public feeds (MeshCore nodes, Meshtastic nodes, FT8 when you ask, and Winlink reports from WINLINK to APWL2K) and keep the last good download on the phone. Details: Map and messages.

7. Retune only a radio you own

The app asks you to confirm. The radio reboots. Use Save as main and Restore main to keep and return a setup. Companion retune is a separate checkbox and does not change transmit power. Details: Remote retune.

Day, Night, OLED, and Auto are the themes. The privacy policy says what stays on the phone.
