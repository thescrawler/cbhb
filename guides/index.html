Guides

Three short guides. The Mesh Tools steps follow the project’s own install page. The app steps follow the MeshCore One Stop guide on this site. The tester steps stop where the Play Store link still has to go.

  • Install Mesh Tools Put the aprs.fi panel on a phone or a computer with Tampermonkey. Version 1.6.3-beta.
  • Use MeshCore One Stop Connect the Android app to your radio, name it on the phone, and read voltage when you ask.
  • Become a tester What to have ready for the Play Store closed test. The join link is not live yet.
