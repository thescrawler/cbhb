Become a tester

We need 12 people to try the Android app for 14 days. That is the closed test Google asks for before the app can move ahead on the Play Store.

The test is not open yet

Play Store closed testing is not live. There is no join link to tap today. When there is one, it will be placed in the box below on this page. Until then, the current sideload build is on the MeshCore One Stop downloads page. Do not install a file someone sends you, and do not use a private shared folder.

What you need

  • A Gmail account. That is the Google email on the phone you will use.
  • An Android phone.

You do not need to be a radio expert. If you can install an app from the Play Store, you can help.

What you will do when the link is ready

  1. Open this page on the Android phone you want to use.
  2. Tap the join link in the box below. It is not there yet.
  3. Sign in with your Gmail account if Google asks.
  4. Join the test.
  5. Install MeshCore One Stop from the Play Store page that Google opens. Use that install button.

Stay in the test for 14 days. We need 12 people to do that.
